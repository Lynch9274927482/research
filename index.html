What is a Research Title?

A research title is a concise statement that tells readers what a study is about. It should clearly represent the focus of the research while remaining specific, understandable, and relevant.

✔ Clear

Readers should immediately understand the topic.

✔ Specific

It identifies the participants, variables, or subject.

✔ Relevant

The title should match the purpose of the study.

✔ Concise

Avoid unnecessary or repetitive words.

✔ Researchable

The topic should be measurable or investigable.
